# OSWorld 2.0: Benchmarking Computer Use Agents on Long-Horizon Real-World Tasks
**Authors / Organization:** Mengqi Yuan et al. — XLANG Lab and collaborators  
**Year / Version:** 2026; arXiv v2  
**Core Contribution (1-2 sentences):** Provides hard evidence that current computer-use agents complete only a minority of realistic long-horizon workflows.  
**Relevance Score (1-10) for Solo Autonomous Business Models:** 10  
**Why this score:** Critical reality check against over-optimism about fully unattended long-horizon agents.

## Key Insights
- Large autonomy gap remains for complex desktop / multi-step computer work
- Short, controlled runs with evaluation still dominate reliable production use

## Actionable Insights for a Solo Founder
- Keep early workflows short-horizon, measurable, and reversible
- Do not depend on open-ended long-horizon computer use for the first micro-ABM
