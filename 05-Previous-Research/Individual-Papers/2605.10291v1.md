# Generative AI Fuels Solo Entrepreneurship, but Teams Still Lead at the Top
**Authors / Organization:** Hyunso Kim; Hyo Kang; Jaeyong Song — Seoul National University Business School  
**Year / Version:** 2026; arXiv v1  
**Core Contribution (1-2 sentences):** Shows generative AI makes solo entry easier, yet teams still dominate the highest-quality outcomes.  
**Relevance Score (1-10) for Solo Autonomous Business Models:** 10  
**Why this score:** Direct evidence on the solo vs team tension that every solo ABM must address.

## Key Insights
- Solo launches increase strongly after broad GenAI access
- Top outcomes remain disproportionately team-based
- Solo founders must deliberately recreate critique, diversity, and accountability through independent agents, customers, or structured review

## Actionable Insights for a Solo Founder
- Design independent review and evaluation into the agent system from day one
