# Agentic Business Process Management: A Research Manifesto
**Authors / Organization:** Diego Calvanese et al. — international BPM / AI collaboration  
**Year / Version:** 2026; arXiv v3  
**Core Contribution (1-2 sentences):** Frames agentic BPM as a sociotechnical system with process-aware agents, explicit frames, actionability, explanation, and controlled adaptation.  
**Relevance Score (1-10) for Solo Autonomous Business Models:** 10  
**Why this score:** Best process-oriented framing for bounded autonomous workflows.

- Agentic Business Process Management (APM)
- Process-aware agents
- Framed autonomy

## Core Frameworks / Models
- Process frames and controlled adaptation

## Practical Patterns & Examples
- Explicit process logic + agent initiative within bounds

## Success Factors & Enablers
- Clear process goals, norms, and authority boundaries

## Limitations, Risks & Failure Modes
- Unframed agents drift and create risk
