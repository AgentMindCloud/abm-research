# AI Is the Strategy: From Agentic AI to Autonomous Business Models and Strategy in the Age of AI
**Authors / Organization:** René Bohnsack; Mickie de Wet  
**Year / Version:** 2025; manuscript / arXiv v2  
**Core Contribution (1-2 sentences):** Defines Autonomous Business Models (ABMs) and presents a progression from agentic AI tools toward AI as the primary executor of value creation, delivery, and capture with minimal ongoing human intervention.  
**Relevance Score (1-10) for Solo Autonomous Business Models:** 10  
**Why this score:** Most direct definition of ABMs, autonomy progression, and synthetic competition relevant to solo founders.

## Key Concepts & Definitions
- Autonomous Business Model (ABM)
- Agentic AI vs fully autonomous execution
- Synthetic competition
- Progressive autonomy

## Core Frameworks / Models
- Autonomy progression ladder
- Value creation / delivery / capture loops owned by agents

## Practical Patterns & Examples
- AI-first strategy where the firm itself is run by agents under founder framing

## Success Factors & Enablers
- Clear goals, measurable outcomes, bounded permissions

## Limitations, Risks & Failure Modes
- Over-claiming full autonomy before reliability exists

## Actionable Insights for a Solo Founder
- Start with narrowly authorized workflows
- Treat AI as the operating system of the business, not just a tool

- Core framing of ABMs as the strategic destination
