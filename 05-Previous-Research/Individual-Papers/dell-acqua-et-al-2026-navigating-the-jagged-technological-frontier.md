# Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier
**Authors:** Fabrizio Dell’Acqua et al.  
**Year / Version:** 2026; Organization Science  
**Core Contribution:** Field experimental evidence on the uneven (jagged) effects of AI on knowledge-worker productivity and quality.  
**Relevance Score:** 10

## Key Insights for Solo ABMs
- AI helps on some tasks and can harm performance on others, even within the same workflow
- Task-level delegation boundaries matter more than blanket “use AI for everything”
- Causal evidence that outside the frontier, AI can produce faster, more coherent mistakes
