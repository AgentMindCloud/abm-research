## 1. Big Picture Goal

Build business models that are **almost fully operated by AI + automation**, run solo by one person (Jani) with the help of AI agents (Hermes local + VPS), connectors, MCPs, etc.

Target: High autonomy, low regulatory burden, digital-first, measurable, and preferably in whitespace that is not already crowded by technical men building for technical men.

Key insight from earlier synthesis:  
The strongest evidence supports **bounded, process-aware autonomy** (explicit state, narrow permissions, independent verification, progressive removal of human approval) rather than unrestricted agent swarms.

## 3. Hard Constraints (updated)

- Operable by one person + AI agents (no permanent team)
- Minimal / zero official permits, licenses, or heavy regulation (Vietnam or international)
- No physical inventory, logistics, or local physical operations requiring staff
- No heavily sanctioned or high-risk financial activities
- Human role: strategy, high-level quality gates, brand, tool acquisition, development, and some operational help (phone/voice ok, video less preferred). 100% hands-off is not the goal — boring.
- Prefer digital / information / agent-service models
- Start with low-to-medium capital; some investment ok if the model is clear
- Avoid: clinical health expertise, childcare for small children, supplements, high-stakes legal/financial products, food products, anything with significant injury or large financial loss risk

## 5. Key Earlier Insights

From the Master Synthesis of the 28 PDFs:

- Best first architecture = Human Governor + framed autonomy + SOP/task graphs + independent review + risk-tiered gates
- Highest autonomizability categories: productized research/monitoring, structured content transformation, narrow software/automation products, bounded support operations, back-office document workflows
- Avoid: unrestricted long-horizon computer use, money movement, clinical/legal decisions, relationship-heavy services without clear escalation

From the Female-majority whitespace research:

- Strongest near-term low-risk candidate was an AI Retention + Margin Operator for independent beauty / esthetics / nail / wellness studios
- Many other ideas (post-discharge care, childcare compliance, clinical copilots) were deprioritized due to regulation and liability
