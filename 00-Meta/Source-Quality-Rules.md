# Source Quality Rules

- Prefer primary sources (official docs, papers, government data, real product pages) over secondary summaries.
- Every claim must have: source name + URL/date + confidence (High / Medium / Low).
- Discard pure marketing claims without evidence of production use.
- Prefer data from 2024–2026. Flag anything older than 2023.
- Note geographic bias (US-heavy data is common).
